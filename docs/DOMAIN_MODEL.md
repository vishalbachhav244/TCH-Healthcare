# Domain model

A facility belongs to a tenant organization with a subscription plan. A facility has versioned inpatient access. A patient has a tenant-local ID. A practitioner is assigned to a facility and specialty. An appointment links a patient and practitioner. A queue ticket points to the checked-in appointment. An encounter identifies a consultation, its practitioner and its signed observation.

An outpatient invoice links a signed encounter, line items and posted payments. A laboratory order links an encounter with an accession, finding, review and publication state. Admissions track bed ownership separately and preserve the allocated bed reference after discharge. A patient session may find records only for its own patient identity and facility.

A connector binds one facility to one recipient identity. A matching code references one patient and connector, expires after 15 minutes and allows one request. A `ConsentGrant` is stored separately from the request and contains a `RecordReference` for the exact released laboratory report version, its expiry and revocation state. Every authorized recipient retrieval attempt with an existing request records a release or denial outcome. The audit projection excludes patient identifiers and report content.

A clinical review references a flagged published report. A referral links that review to a facility quality case. The quality register exposes a process title, category and priority. Case events preserve the actor, timestamp and status, with a structured disposition for resolution.

A waitlist request links one patient, clinician and facility date. It has its own lifecycle and version, and links to an appointment only after successful conversion. An unreleased schedule block excludes a clinician's slots over a half-open time range. Releasing a block preserves its record. Booking, rescheduling and waitlist conversion check the same appointment and block stores in their write transaction. [Scheduling contracts](../src/app/domain/scheduling.ts)

A follow-up request links a signed encounter to a patient, clinician, facility and date window. Booking creates a new appointment and advances the request version atomically. Staff cancellation releases the request for another booking while the cancelled appointment retains its original request link. The clinical projection includes signed observations and the follow-up purpose. The booking projection includes prior clinician metadata and scheduling fields. [Continuity contracts](../src/app/domain/continuity.ts)

A `WardAssignment` links an active admission to a facility physician and nurse. Rounds, orders and observations belong to that admission. A ward order has one `NursingTask`, whose status also drives the order view. Completing a task and recording its linked observation occur in one transaction. Assignment, admission and task versions guard stale writes. Open tasks follow the current nursing assignment and block discharge. Rounds and observations remain stored after discharge. [Ward-care contracts](../src/app/domain/ward-care.ts)

An `EmergencyCase` links the arrival and clinician-entered assessment to an optional admission. `IcuReview` and `IcuStepDown` reference the existing admission, while ICU membership follows its occupied critical care bed. A non-ICU transfer resolves a step-down request. A `TheatreCase` links one admission to a room and dated interval. Open cases prevent discharge until completed or cancelled. Case versions enforce valid transitions. [Acute care contracts](../src/app/domain/acute-care.ts)

A `DischargePlan` references one admission, facility and patient. Draft writes and signature check the admission, team and plan versions. Signature locks the summary. Discharge releases a snapshot of the patient and facility names, final placement and dates. The patient projection requires the same facility, patient and completed admission. An administrative admission without a care team can close without a summary.

A `BedStay` records the start and end of an occupied bed segment and its ward rate. At discharge, the admission’s segments are closed and converted to a frozen `InpatientAccount`. Each occupied IST calendar day uses the highest recorded ward rate that day. Inpatient invoices reference the admission and have a unique admission index. They use the existing invoice line, payment and patient balance model. [Discharge and billing contracts](../src/app/domain/discharge.ts)

An `ImagingOrder` links an assigned encounter, patient and facility to a catalogue study and one modality booking. A room and patient can each have one active booking per slot. The technician records acquisition. The reviewer records a synthetic report summary and confirms its release. Published reports expose only their summary, study, release time and reviewer to the patient. [Imaging contracts](../src/app/domain/radiology.ts)

A `BloodRequest` links the assigned physician’s order to an active admission and facility. A facility-scoped `BloodUnit` has a component, expiry day and one request in custody at a time. Reservation, release and issue update request and unit together. The review is a recorded synthetic decision. Available and reserved units are checked against the facility-local date before custody changes. [Blood bank contracts](../src/app/domain/blood-bank.ts)

A pharmacy lot records a facility, catalogue item, batch and expiry. Its opening balance, receipts, dispensing and supplier returns determine the on-hand quantity. Patient returns reference the dispensed prescription, lot and original invoice. They add only to quarantine and carry the calculated invoice credit and any amount requiring payment review. The invoice and previous settlement records retain their original values. [Pharmacy contracts](../src/app/domain/pharmacy.ts)

A `RefundDecision` resolves one paid medication return review. A recorded refund reduces net collections on the posting day and leaves the original invoice and payment as recorded. A `CollectionClose` stores the expected ledger totals and matching count for one facility and IST day. [Finance contracts](../src/app/domain/finance.ts)

`InsuranceCoverage` belongs to a patient and facility. An `InsuranceClaim` binds eligible coverage to one consultation or inpatient invoice. The claim keeps the requested and approved amounts and a version for transitions. An open claim holds patient collection. A settled claim posts one invoice payment with the claim ID and releases the hold. [Insurance contracts](../src/app/domain/insurance.ts)

A `Requisition` identifies the requesting facility, catalogue item, quantity and approval state. A `PurchaseOrder` references one approved requisition and records its supplier and quoted cost. A `GoodsReceipt` links the purchase order, lot and stock movement. Receipt posting increments the order and the lot together. A stock count records the difference between the expected on-hand quantity and the physical count. It preserves quarantine as a separate balance. [Supply contracts](../src/app/domain/procurement.ts)

A `StaffShift` records a facility membership version, staff account, local date, planned times and recorded attendance timestamps. Historical shifts retain the assignment used when they were created. The roster computes whether that assignment is current. [Workforce contracts](../src/app/domain/workforce.ts)

A `BiomedicalAsset` has a facility-unique tag and may link to one imaging room. A `MaintenanceOrder` references the asset and records its status, last actor and functional check. An active order locks availability. Cancellation or release updates both records in one transaction. [Biomedical contracts](../src/app/domain/biomedical.ts)

A `SupportTask` records a request, ward, status and version. For Housekeeping it also links a bed, with a unique active index that clears when work completes. Task completion and bed release share a transaction. [Hospital support contracts](../src/app/domain/support.ts)

A `TransportRequest` links an admission, patient, pickup bed and, after dispatch, a facility `TransportUnit`. Pickup and handoff are separate states. Unit availability is computed from the active assignments. [Patient transport contracts](../src/app/domain/transport.ts)

An `Enquiry` links a tenant patient to a facility, capture source, specialty and optional agent. Contact attempts form a dated history. The current appointment relationship drives the booked state and source conversion. An `EngagementPermission` stores patient opt-in per facility and channel. A `Campaign` stores a template, lifecycle version and aggregate simulation result. [Engagement contracts](../src/app/domain/engagement.ts)

An `AnalyticsReport` contains dated totals and specialty aggregates. Wait sample counts and elapsed time support a weighted period mean. A facility `DocumentationPolicy` controls generation and the daily quota. A `DocumentationDraft` references the assigned encounter, author, format, version and review state. Review is separate from the encounter lifecycle. [Intelligence contracts](../src/app/domain/intelligence.ts)

A `PatientDocument` belongs to one patient and facility. Uploads reference an immutable synthetic fixture. Facility releases reference a signed encounter and the recorded visit details. A consent grant identifies the record type, ID and version. [Document contracts](../src/app/domain/patient-documents.ts)

The TypeScript contracts live in [operations.ts](../src/app/domain/operations.ts), [diagnostics.ts](../src/app/domain/diagnostics.ts), [portal.ts](../src/app/domain/portal.ts), [interoperability.ts](../src/app/domain/interoperability.ts) and [governance.ts](../src/app/domain/governance.ts). Imported record references and broader external identity verification remain open.
