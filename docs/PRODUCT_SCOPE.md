# Product scope

[Enterprise frontend brief](ENTERPRISE_FRONTEND_BRIEF.md) is the sole product specification. This workspace begins from newly generated Angular files. The brief lists 36 dashboard IDs and references baseline counts of 28 modules, 137 screens and 171 requirements. The individual baseline screen and requirement identifiers are absent from the brief. Only routes and workflow portions implemented here are counted in the register.

Current scope covers platform administration, hospital overview, appointment and queue management, patient registry, clinician worklist, laboratory, inpatient bed workflow, outpatient billing and a patient portal. The [dashboard registry](DASHBOARD_REGISTRY.md) records the remaining responsibilities.
